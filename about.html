The striped awning, the shop window, the printing signs: this photograph makes a business feel human. The number 108 is visible above the doorway. We do not have a confirmed date or names for the people pictured.

Some chapters have clearer records. Paducah Printing’s own history traces its roots to 1860. Later sources give us names, an address, a production milestone, and a place in the city’s printed memory. [1]

The record, one chapter at a time

A history you can follow.

  1. 1860

    The earliest chapter

    Roots in western Kentucky.

    Paducah Printing dates its history to 1860 and describes a long connection with communication products in western Kentucky. That is the company’s own account of its beginnings. [1]

  2. 1963

    A family chapter

    The Shulman name enters the story.

    A 2016 supplier report records Charles Shulman’s purchase of Paducah Printing in 1963. The same report identifies his son, Bruce Shulman, as president at the time it was published. [2]

  3. 1967

    An address in print

    A small ad leaves a lasting trace.

    The February 23, 1967 issue of Lone Oak High School’s Oak “K” carries a Paducah Printing Company advertisement with a familiar address and phone number: 233 North 8th and 443-5383. Both remain part of the company’s contact details today. [3] [4]

    PADUCAH PRINTING COMPANY
    233 N. 8th
    Ph. 443-5383

    Advertisement text, Oak “K”, February 23, 1967

    A transcript of the advertisement, rather than a reproduction of the newspaper page.

  4. 2016

    The tools change

    A new press opens another chapter.

    In August 2016, RMGT reported a February installation of a new 9 Series LED-UV press at Paducah Printing. The account describes a move from a smaller press format to producing 16-page signatures for publication work. [2]

  5. 2020

    Printing the city’s memory

    Paducah’s history, bound into a book.

    The 2020 Paducah Wall to Wall: Portraits of Our Past publication credits Paducah Printing Corporation with its printing. A collection devoted to the city’s floodwall murals became another local story carried on paper. [5]

    Explore the publication and its printer credit

  6. Today

    The next sheet is yours

    Print, design, and mail under one roof.

    Paducah Printing brings printing and finishing together with design through Horizon Media Group and mailing through Mailit Xpress. The work spans digital, offset, and wide format printing, with folding, saddle binding, foil, embossing, and die cutting among the options. [6]

Read the sources and photograph notes
  1. Paducah Printing, History. The company states that its history dates to 1860. Other records use different establishment dates: a 2016 trade release uses 1877, while a Kentucky business directory lists 1907. The records reviewed do not explain the difference, so this page attributes 1860 to the company while leaving the different establishment dates open for further research.
  2. RMGT 9Series, August 4, 2016. A supplier-published account supports the 1963 purchase and 2016 installation. Historical leadership, equipment, and certification statements in that report are not treated as current claims.
  3. Oak “K”, February 23, 1967. Digitized school newspaper hosted by the Lone Oak alumni archive; the advertisement is on the fourth PDF page.
  4. Paducah Printing, Contact. Current published address, telephone number, and business hours.
  5. Paducah Wall to Wall, 2020 publication. Copyright year on the title page; printer credit on printed page 118, the 119th PDF page. The publication’s images are copyrighted and are not reproduced here.
  6. Paducah Printing homepage and capability page. Sources for the present service overview.
