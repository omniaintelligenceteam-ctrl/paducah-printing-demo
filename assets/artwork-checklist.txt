PADUCAH PRINTING — ARTWORK CHECKLIST

Before exporting
[ ] Confirm finished dimensions, quantity, material, finishing, and deadline with the team.
[ ] Ask which bleed, safe area, crop marks, and PDF preset the particular job needs.
[ ] Proofread names, dates, prices, contact details, and page order.
[ ] Use high-resolution source images. Around 300 dpi at final size is a common target for close-viewed print; ask about wide-format work.
[ ] Discuss CMYK/grayscale, spot colors, and color-critical artwork with prepress.
[ ] Check font embedding and any linked assets in your exported PDF.

Before sending
[ ] Export the agreed press-ready PDF and inspect every page.
[ ] Check page size, bleed, margins, and image quality.
[ ] Keep foil, embossing, and cutting instructions clearly identified; confirm setup with the team.
[ ] Include your job description, quantity, requested deadline, and P.O. reference if applicable.
[ ] Follow current size and file-type requirements at the upload portal.
