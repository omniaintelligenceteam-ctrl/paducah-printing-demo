01 / Everyday essentials

Business printing

Put a consistent face on your business, from the first handshake to the next order. Business cards, stationery, forms, flyers, and other everyday pieces keep your name moving.

Digital and offset printing offer different ways to meet your quantity, color, and budget needs. We can talk through the right approach, paper, and proofing for your job.

Start a business print brief

02 / Room for the whole story

Brochures & booklets

Give your reader something to explore. Folded brochures, catalogs, programs, and booklets bring information together in a form people can hold, share, and return to.

Folding and saddle binding help turn printed sheets into a finished piece. Discuss page count, finished size, paper, and binding before your layout is final.

Get your files ready

03 / Think bigger

Signs & banners

Make the message visible across a room or at your next event. Wide format printing gives signs, banners, posters, and display graphics space to speak.

Tell us where the piece will go, how it will be displayed, and how long you need it to last. Those details help guide the material, size, and finishing conversation.

Plan a larger piece

04 / Put it in their hands

Direct mail

Turn a printed piece into a direct connection. Postcards, letters, and other mailed materials can carry an offer, introduce a business, or keep a community informed.

Mailing services through Mailit Xpress connect print with the next step. Ask about variable data printing for personalized content, and confirm mailpiece dimensions and addressing requirements before designing.

Talk through a mailing

05 / The finishing touch

Specialty finishes

A little texture or an unexpected shape can change how a piece feels. Explore foil stamping, embossing, and die cutting for invitations, presentation pieces, and more.

These details work best when planned early. Bring us your idea before finalizing artwork so we can discuss paper, placement, tooling, and file preparation.

Explore a finishing idea

06 / Start with an idea

Design

You do not need a finished file to start a conversation. Design services through Horizon Media Group can help shape your message into a piece that is ready for print.

Bring the words, images, and purpose you have. We will discuss what the project needs, from a new layout to preparing existing artwork for production.
