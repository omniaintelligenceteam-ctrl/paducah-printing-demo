Before we talk

Good questions to bring.

What do you need for an estimate?

Start with the type of piece, quantity, finished size, number of pages or printed sides, and your target date. Paper, color, finishing, design, and mailing needs help fill in the picture. It is fine if some details are still undecided.

Does downloading a brief send my project to you?

No. The brief is prepared on this device for you to keep or copy. Call 270-443-5383 to discuss the project and request an estimate. Send artwork separately through the official upload portal.

Can you help if I only have an idea?

Yes. Tell us what the piece needs to do and what material you already have. We can discuss print options and design services through Horizon Media Group.

How do I confirm a delivery date?

Call to discuss the schedule for your specific job. Quantity, artwork readiness, proofing, materials, and finishing all belong in that conversation. The date in your project brief is a request.
